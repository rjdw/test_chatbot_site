FAQ

What tech stack runs this site?

Cloudflare Pages + Tailwind CSS + Vite + Three.js (for the Klein bottle scroll journey) + a Cloudflare Worker that proxies Google Gemini for the chat widget.

How does the Klein-bottle scroll thing work?

A WebGL canvas renders Hermann Karcher's twisting Klein bottle using its parametric equations. Your scroll position maps to a parameter along a curve inside the surface, so each scroll step advances the camera through the bottle's non-orientable loop. Two full laps are spread across the page, which is why exiting on one side brings you back flipped through the other.

Can I reuse your code?

Nope, unless otherwise specified. Some stuff is MIT license.
